Holy FYP privacy policy

Holy FYP is developed by Seth Parsons. This policy describes the app’s local reading features, optional online services and developer contact.

On-device reading data

Reading position, saved items, appearance and audio preferences, video consent, and limited usage counts/day timestamps for occasional rating and support reminders are stored on your device. Holy FYP does not send your saved Scripture, bookmarks or reading position to the developer or YouTube. The app has no Holy FYP account, analytics SDK or developer-operated user-data server. Your device’s backup settings may apply to local app data.

Age-range check for videos

On iOS 26 or later, Holy FYP requests an age range through Apple’s Declared Age Range framework before enabling YouTube. Videos are disabled for users under 13 and when an eligible age range is not shared or cannot be determined. On earlier iOS versions, YouTube is unavailable. Bible reading, quotations and music remain available. The app does not ask for your birth date. The returned range is used locally to make the eligibility decision, is not uploaded or persisted by Holy FYP, and eligibility is rechecked after the app returns from the background. Apple handles its own age-sharing service and controls.

Optional YouTube playback

If you enable videos, Holy FYP loads the official YouTube embedded player in a web view using youtube-nocookie.com and temporary website storage. Visible clips may play automatically after your video choice. Google/YouTube receives network and player information, which may include your IP address, device/browser information, video requests, playback interactions, diagnostics and identifiers used by its services. The player may show advertising. Privacy-enhanced mode limits personalization; it does not make playback anonymous or eliminate all data collection.

Google/YouTube controls its processing and retention under the Google Privacy Policy. See also YouTube’s Terms of Service and information about embedded players. Holy FYP does not request your YouTube credentials. You can decline videos or turn them off in Settings. This does not prevent offline reading.

Public video catalogue and website hosting

The app periodically requests a public list of available video links from GitHub Pages. These requests do not include your saved readings or an app account identifier. As the hosting provider, GitHub may process technical request information such as IP addresses and server logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The support and privacy webpages are hosted there too. No analytics scripts are added to these pages.

Music, narration and purchases

Bundled music plays locally. Read-aloud uses Apple’s device speech services. Optional tips are handled by Apple’s In-App Purchase system; the developer does not receive your payment card details. The app stores a local acknowledgment of verified tips to show thanks and avoid a support reminder. Apple handles its own purchase/account information under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Contacting the developer

If you choose Email the developer, the app prepares an editable email with app/build version, generic device/hardware model and iOS version. It does not send automatically and does not include a personal device name or unique device identifier. If you send the email, the developer receives your email address, message and any diagnostics or attachments you choose to include. These are used to respond and troubleshoot, retained as needed for that purpose, and not sold or used for advertising. Contact the developer to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to necessary legal or recordkeeping requirements.

Your choices and outside links

You may change motion, music, narration and video preferences in Settings, remove saved items, or delete the app to remove its local data. There is no Holy FYP account to delete. Opening YouTube, the App Store, music sources or other external links uses the destination’s services and privacy practices. The app is not designed specifically for young children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer, contact us so it can be addressed.

Contact and updates
