Holy FYP support

Your FYP Found Jesus. Holy FYP is a free iPhone app for reading Scripture, historic Christian quotations and clearly labeled reflections.

Contact the developer

Email sethparsons6@icloud.com for help, feedback, content concerns or privacy questions. You can also use Settings → Contact developer in the app. Please describe what happened and include your app version and iOS version. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Reading and saved items

Tap a reading for context or sources. Double-tap to save. Saved items, preferences and reading progress are stored on your device; Holy FYP has no account or developer-operated cloud sync. Deleting the app removes its local data. Your device’s own backup settings may also apply.

Music and motion

Settings includes background music, read-aloud, moving colors, text size and appearance controls. Music and narration are alternatives and pause for videos. Reduce Motion is respected. Music credits and original source links are available in Settings.

Optional videos

YouTube clips require a connection and the app’s video privacy choice. You may decline or turn off videos and continue reading offline. Clips can be unavailable because of network, regional or publisher restrictions. The official YouTube player may show ads. Holy FYP does not download YouTube media.

Optional tips

All features are free. Small, Medium and Large tips are optional, repeatable one-time purchases through Apple, not subscriptions. They unlock no content. Cancelling a purchase does not affect reading. If prices do not load, check your connection and try again later. For billing or refund requests, use Apple’s purchase support.

Scripture and sources

Scripture uses the public-domain World English Bible. Tap passages for surrounding Scripture and quotations for source information. Questions and reflections are original editorial material, distinct from Scripture. Please email specific references or quotation details if you spot an issue.
